PLATFORM & TEAM

Build the platform and the team around it.

I hired and manage three full-time data engineers, developed from analysts, and direct about ten contract engineers. I lead our ETL modernization from SAP Data Services and Informatica to dbt and Matillion on Snowflake, and designed a production behavioral-event pipeline with medallion modeling and incremental ingestion. COST & SPEED

Make the platform cheaper and the team faster.

I cut Snowflake compute 20% and storage 50% within several months by resolving high-spill queries, optimizing writes, removing redundant data copies, and improving data models. I led department-wide adoption of AI-assisted development and QA; a vendor analysis of logged time found cycle time on comparable tickets fell from about five days to one, a projected 2,000 hours a year.

Cost optimization · performance · AI-assisted development

Pluribus

Follow the government decisions that affect you, with a source behind every claim.

Pluribus brings Miami-Dade residents’ representatives, bills, ballots, and elections into one app built on official sources. I designed the product, built the app, and built the data pipelines that pull and reconcile civic data from several providers.

What I owned

  • Defined the product and chose a deep Florida and Miami-Dade first release over thin national coverage
  • Built the React Native (Expo) app and its main flows
  • Built GraphQL ingestion of ballot and legislative data and an incremental-sync bill importer as Supabase edge functions
  • Took it through App Store review to launch

Proof it works

  • Bills sync incrementally from official sources
